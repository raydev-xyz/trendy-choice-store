How this works

The shop and this panel read the same settings document, so a change here is live on the shop the next time a page loads. Products themselves always come from the BusinessKoro catalogue — this panel decides which of them the shop shows, in what order, and at what price.

Products

Tick Show to list a product in the shop. Pin puts it at the top of the shop, in the order you pin them. Change the price to override the catalogue price for this store.

New Arrival slider

Pick the products that appear in the New Arrival slider on the home page. They are shown in the order you pick them, with their big product photo. Leave it empty and the shop falls back to the newest products in the catalogue.

Banner images

Paste image URLs, one per line. They are used as the store banner in the order listed. Use a direct image link (ending .jpg, .png or .webp).

Orders

Every order the shop placed, with the payment details the customer entered. Hide removes an order from this board without touching the order in BusinessKoro.
